The Pipeline

1. Content

LLM-driven outline and chapter generation with genre-specific templates and citation scrubbing — unbacked claims are removed, not hallucinated.

2. Formatting

Programmatic EPUB and print-ready PDF with automated validation against KDP upload specs: file size, metadata, link integrity, image dimensions.

3. Cover

Genre-aware template engine producing 1600×2560 KDP-standard covers at zero marginal cost. GPU-backed photoreal covers are the next upgrade.

4. Packaging

One-command KDP ZIP bundle (EPUB + PDF + cover + metadata) and a ready-to-sell Gumroad listing.
