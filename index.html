Aether Labs turns one topic into a publication-ready ebook: AI-drafted chapters, a formatted EPUB and print PDF, a genre-matched cover, a KDP upload package, and a Gumroad listing — from a single command, in minutes.

Who it's for: indie authors and publishers who want a manuscript-to-market pipeline without hiring a formatter or learning InDesign. Not for: people who want a tool that writes the book and does nothing else.

Built because the part of self-publishing that kills most authors isn't the writing — it's the 60–120 hours of formatting, cover design, and upload compliance that comes after it. This engine does that part.

The pipeline

1 · Draft

Outline and chapters generated from genre templates; unsupported claims are stripped, not hallucinated. Runs offline on the bundled corpus.

2 · Format

Validated EPUB and print-ready PDF that pass KDP's file-size, metadata, and image checks before you ever upload.

3 · Cover

Genre-aware covers at 1600×2560, built to KDP spec. Photoreal GPU-backed covers land in v2.

4 · Ship

One command produces the KDP ZIP and a Gumroad listing. You review, you publish.

Output formats

EPUB 3 Print-ready PDF KDP upload ZIP Gumroad listing 1600×2560 cover SQLite catalog
